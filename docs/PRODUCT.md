# PRODUCT.md — There & Back

## Vision
The app that saves parents on trips. A collection of offline mini-games
for toddlers aged 2–5, themed around travel and adventure. The mascot Nino
guides children through games with animations and sounds, no reading required.

## Target audience
- **Primary player:** 2–3 year olds (designed for 2)
- **Primary buyer:** parents traveling with toddlers
- **Purchase context:** airport, airplane, long car rides
- **Key insight:** parent is a desperate buyer in this context.
  They will pay immediately if the app works for their child.

## Reference apps to study before any feature work
| App | What to learn |
|---|---|
| Sago Mini Friends | Zero fail states, pure exploration |
| Sago Mini Doodleland | Drawing mechanics for toddlers |
| Toca Kitchen | Open-ended play, no wrong answers |
| Bimi Boo | Mini-game collection structure, wood aesthetic, IAP model |
| Endless Alphabet | Audio-first, character reactions to everything |

## MVP scope — Game 1: Memory Match
- 6 pairs of travel-themed cards (12 cards total)
- Cards look like wooden puzzle pieces (Skia wood grain)
- Nino reacts: floats at top, celebrates matches, gentle "oops" on mismatch
- No timer, no lives, no score pressure
- Win condition: all pairs found → big Nino celebration (Lottie) + sound
